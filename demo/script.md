# Onze Plek: 30-second demo script

The video has no sound; one speaker talks over it. Start talking as soon as the video starts.
Each line begins when you **see** its cue, so you don't need to watch a clock.
It is about 75 words, a calm pace for 30 seconds. Add the words in [brackets] only if you have time.

| Time | You see | You say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | A phone camera on the sticker | "Every container gets this sticker. Scan it: no app needed." |
| 0:03 | The container page opens: *Space left, push the flap* | "It opens this container. Remember the 4%? Not full: push the flap." |
| 0:07 | A tap on *Bags next to it* | "Something wrong? One tap reports it. No cameras, no fines." |
| 0:11 | A tap on *I want to host this spot*, then *Set as my spot* | "72% said nobody feels responsible. Now one neighbour does: the Spot Host." |
| 0:17 | *My spot*: 24 of 30 clean days | "My spot: clean on 24 of the last 30 days." |
| 0:20 | Buurtbonus: *24 of 26 clean days, goal based on your own start* | "Beat your own start, and the block earns the Buurtbonus." |
| 0:22 | A vote for *Plants on the container plate* | "Neighbours choose the reward, sponsored by local partners and the city. [A reward for the street, not for one person.]" |
| 0:27–0:30 | The screen holds | Pause, then go to the next slide. |

## Before the pitch

- Put the video on the slide set to **play automatically** with no loop. Have the speaker click to the slide and start talking at once.
- Rehearse 3 times with the video playing. Then the cues come on their own.
- If a juror asks "Did my vote count?": votes, reports and hosting stay on their own phone. It is a prototype, so nothing is sent to us or the city.
- If the video won't play: open the live site on a phone (or scan the sticker) and do the same 4 taps by hand.
